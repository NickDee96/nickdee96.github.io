Recent work

  • Microsoft Research Africa (Aug 2025 – Nov 2026). Speech AI for African languages in Project Gecko. I trained multilingual speech-recognition models from scratch, built text-to-speech with reinforcement-learning post-training, and built the data pipeline and co-developed the evaluation harness behind PazaBench, a public benchmark of 51–52 ASR models across 39 African languages (announcement). Our paper on PazaBench was accepted for an oral presentation at Deep Learning Indaba 2026.
  • GovBot. Main engineer on the AI assistant for Kenya's eCitizen government-services portal, built at THiNK under the GovStack initiative with GIZ, ITU and ICTA (code).
  • Brave Venture Labs (2018 – present). Data scientist, then machine learning engineer. I built LLM products with evaluation against ground-truth labels and cost monitoring, and the backend of UnlockAI, a teaching assistant now reaching more classrooms through changeforce.
  • Ecobank Kenya (2023). Led development of Rafiki, the bank's customer-service chatbot, which cut calls to the Kenyan contact centre by 70%.
  • Hugging Face. Swahili, Kikuyu and Dholuo speech, translation and dataset releases (nickdee96, thinkKenya).
